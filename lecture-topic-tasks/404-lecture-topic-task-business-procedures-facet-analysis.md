## 🎯 Objective
Document the Business Procedures Facet of the pronunciation coaching domain. This facet focuses on long, regularly performed, domain-relevant procedures followed by stakeholders such as learners, instructors, and evaluators. The goal is to capture the workflows and processes used in pronunciation learning and teaching.

---

## 📝 Description
The Business Procedures facet describes long, regularly performed workflows that stakeholders follow in the pronunciation coaching domain. These are real-world processes that show how learners carry out pronunciation learning. 

This section should document core domain procedures such as:
- How learners prepare for practice
- How they attempt and refine pronunciation
- How they interpret feedback
- How they improve

Each procedure must be expressed as a step-by-step workflow, similar to the examples in the slides (“plan trip → buy ticket → board train”). These procedures help clarify how the domain operates and support analysis of other facets.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Identify at least 3–5 business procedures relevant to our domain
- [ ] Each procedure is presented as a sequence of steps 
- [ ] Procedures are relevant to the domain, regularly performed, and long-winded

---

## 🧪 Testing Plan
- Confirm that each workflow is realistic
- Check that each procedure describes ordered, multi-step behaviors
