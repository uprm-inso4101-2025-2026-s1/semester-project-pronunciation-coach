## 🎯 Objective
Perform a guideword-based analysis for the domain risk “User Loses Motivation Over Time.”
Identify hazards using the guidewords NO, MORE, LESS, EARLY, LATE, and WRONG, and analyze how each can lead to reduced engagement, avoidance behavior, or abandonment of pronunciation practice. 

---

## 📝 Description
This analysis applies the guidewords from the “Fundamentals of RE” lecture. Instead of trees, we use systematic guidewords to analyze the events that can lead to motivation loss in our pronunciation coach application.

Guidewords to apply:
- NO (no reward, no feedback, no progress)
- LESS (less clarity, less encouragement, less variation)
- MORE (more criticism, more difficulty, more noise)
- EARLY (feedback too early)
- LATE (delayed scoring, slow responses, late reminders)
- WRONG (wrong scoring, wrong alignment, wrong feedback)
