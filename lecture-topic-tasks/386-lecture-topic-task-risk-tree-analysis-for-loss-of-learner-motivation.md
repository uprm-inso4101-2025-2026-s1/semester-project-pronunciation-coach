## Details
## 🎯 Objective
Create a risk tree analysis focused on the domain risk of the user‘s motivation loss during the pronunciation practice. Identify how user frustration, feedback failures, technical issues, and behavioral patterns can lead to decreased practice frequency, abandonment of the app, or ineffective learning.

---

## 📝 Description
Loss of learner motivation is one of the biggest risks in the pronunciation coach domain.
If learners stop practicing, the system fails its core objective: improving the user pronunciation 

Based on the “Risk Trees & Evaluation” lecture, the analysis should include:
- A root event (loss of motivation)
- AND/OR nodes
- Leaf nodes such as mis-scoring, delays, erorrs, lost of streaks or confusing feedback
- Mitigation strategies
