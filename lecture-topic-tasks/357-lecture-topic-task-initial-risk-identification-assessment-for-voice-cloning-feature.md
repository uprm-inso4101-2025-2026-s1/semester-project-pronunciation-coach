## Details
## 🎯 Objective
Identify and assess the major risks in the voice-cloning feature used to generate corrected pronunciations in the user’s own voice.

---

## 📝 Description
The app will use OpenVoice to clone the user’s voice and play corrected pronunciations. This process depends on audio input quality, stable model behavior, and consistent preprocessing. We need a documented list of key product and process risks—along with likelihood and severity ratings—to understand which parts of the pipeline are most vulnerable and require mitigation.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Identify at least 10 relevant risks, split into product/process
- [ ] Assign each risk a likelihood (Likely / Possible / Unlikely)
- [ ] Assign each risk a severity (High / Moderate / Low)
- [ ] Include a risk matrix highlighting which risks are highest priority
- [ ] Clearly specify which risks require immediate mitigation in future issues

---

## 🧪 Testing Plan
How will this be tested?  
- Review risks with the team for completeness
- Validate likelihood/severity assignments through developer discussion
